NOBERTRADES SITE V2
====================
Upload the contents of this folder to Cloudflare Workers & Pages -> your static worker.

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js

The site includes:
Home/Landing page, market terminal mockup, trading tools/calculators,
EA/Bot store, signals section, Academy, pricing, WhatsApp/email contact.

IMPORTANT:
The market prices shown in the visual terminal are demo/static values. They are NOT live market data.
The calculators are client-side utilities. Validate formulas for your broker/symbol specifications before using them for real trades.
